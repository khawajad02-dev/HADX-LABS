## Findings applied to HADX LABS

- Motion should have a clear trigger, rule, feedback response, and loop/mode; avoid animation that exists only for decoration.
- Product cards benefit from subtle image zoom, contextual reveal, and a clear discovery action without obscuring the product.
- Media galleries benefit from smooth transitions and thumbnail feedback that encourages exploration.
- Premium fashion pages commonly use restrained motion, deliberate spacing, strong imagery, and editorial sequencing rather than many unrelated effects.
- HADX direction: use restrained lightning-reactive borders, press compression, image depth/zoom, a signal sweep on hover, and a short add-to-cart confirmation. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and keep mobile taps responsive.
